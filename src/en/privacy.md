# Privacy Policy

**Paint Taps: Liquid Tube Puzzle**
Effective date: 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Paint Taps mobile game ("the Game", "we", "us") uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Aydin Coskunirmak, an independent developer. If you have any questions, contact us at **csknrmk.aydn@icloud.com**.

## 1. The short version

- Paint Taps is a single-player game. You do not need an account and we do not ask for your real name, email or phone number.
- Your progress is saved on your device. For the leaderboard we store only a nickname and your level count, linked to a random ID.
- We collect anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, which levels are hard) to improve the Game. We do not use your advertising ID for this.
- Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
- Ads, if shown, are provided by Google AdMob, which may use device data as described below.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your levels, stars, coins, jokers, daily gift and chest progress, and settings (sound, vibration, notifications) on your device. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

## 3. Leaderboard

When you open the Game, Firebase Authentication (Google) gives your device a random anonymous ID. No sign-in is needed. Your nickname (a random one such as "Player4821" until you change it) and the number of levels you have completed are stored with this ID in Google Cloud Firestore and shown to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your nickname.

## 4. Gameplay statistics

We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how the Game is played. It records events such as level started, completed, failed or left, time spent, stars, drains, hints and jokers used, together with your app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version, country and approximate region (derived from your IP address). This data is not linked to your name and we do not use the advertising ID (IDFA) for it. We use it only in aggregate to balance levels and fix problems. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## 5. Purchases

You can buy coins, jokers or other items through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. The store tells the Game on your device that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered. We never receive or store your payment card details.

## 6. Advertising

The Game may show ads, including optional rewarded ads, provided by **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (on iOS only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data. Where required, we ask for your consent first. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can limit ad tracking at any time: on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## 7. Game Center and Google Play Games

If you use **Game Center** (iOS) or **Google Play Games** (Android), your player name, avatar and scores are shared through those services and may be visible to other players. These services are operated by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

## 8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game may send reminders (for example, when your daily gift is ready). These are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or in your device settings.

## 9. Crash and diagnostic data

If you have agreed to share diagnostics with Apple or Google, they may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you personally.

## 10. How we use information

- To run the Game, save your progress and show the leaderboard.
- To see which levels are too hard or too easy and improve the Game.
- To deliver and restore purchases.
- To show ads and grant rewards for rewarded ads.
- To fix problems and improve the Game.
- To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 11. Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

Where data protection laws apply, data is processed to provide the Game you requested (performance of a contract), for our legitimate interests (keeping the Game working and secure), and based on your consent where required (for example, personalized ads and tracking). You can withdraw consent at any time.

## 12. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the Game:

- **Apple** – app distribution, purchases, Game Center.
- **Google** – app distribution, purchases, Google Play Games, AdMob advertising, and Firebase (anonymous sign-in, leaderboard database and Analytics).

These providers may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards. We may disclose information if required by law.

## 13. Retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. Your leaderboard entry (nickname and level count) is kept while the Game is offered; you can ask us to delete it at any time by email. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Data held by Apple or Google for their own services is managed under their policies.

## 14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at **csknrmk.aydn@icloud.com**. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority – KVKK).

## 15. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 16. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
